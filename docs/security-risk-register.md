# AI Security Risk Register

Last reviewed: **10 October 2026**. Control baseline: security hardening
`3320708` and CI quota alignment `80c3482`. Source references identify the
enforcement points in the current workspace and were rechecked for this register
update. The cited deployment evidence remains specific to `80c3482`.

Ratings are qualitative for the synthetic-data demo and must be reassessed before
production data or users are introduced. Existing residual ratings and owners are
retained. The "Low after activation" ratings for AI-17 and AI-18 are conditional
targets requiring verified infrastructure activation, not unconditional current
acceptance. This review grants no new risk acceptance. Newly registered AI-19
through AI-22 have provisional inherent ratings and require owner assessment of
their residual exposure; their scenarios are not confirmed exploits.

## Acceptance Rules

- No Critical residual risk is accepted for the demo.
- Any new real-data, identity, persistent-memory, external-tool, or transaction capability triggers a risk reassessment. This register explicitly includes the existing backend record-writing capability.
- Critical guardrail and authorization controls require automated tests and cannot be waived by a prompt change.
- Accepted High residual risks require a named owner, expiry date, and documented approval in the final project risk log. The AI-03 High rating remains; this update does not create that approval.
- Deployment or offline-evaluation success cannot close an incomplete live safety/usefulness assessment or an unverified infrastructure activation item.
- AI-19 through AI-22 require documented residual assessment and an explicit activation/acceptance decision before real-data or production use; provisional ratings and proposed owner roles are not approvals.
- Finding closure records must state the verified scope, closure date and approver. Accepted exceptions retain their review/expiry requirements and cannot be recorded as remediated solely because a release gate passed.
