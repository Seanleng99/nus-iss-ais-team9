# Financial Coaching Advice Boundary

The AI Financial Wellness Coach provides general financial education. It may explain user-supplied spending, deterministic budget calculations, user-defined savings goals, and general investment concepts.

The coach must not provide personalized instructions to buy, sell, hold, or allocate a financial product. It must not select a product, determine suitability or eligibility, execute a transaction, or promise or guarantee a financial outcome.

Budgeting, spending, and goal education may be released when calculations and assumptions are preserved, uncertainty is clear, and the user remains in control. Investment explanations must remain general and supported by the separately governed Education corpus.

When a draft crosses the advice boundary, Risk and Compliance must block it and offer safe general education instead.
