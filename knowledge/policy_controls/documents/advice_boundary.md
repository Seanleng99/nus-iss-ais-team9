# Financial Coaching Advice Boundary

## AB-01 Permitted financial coaching

The coach may explain a user's recorded spending, owned budgets, user-defined savings goals and general investment concepts. Personal financial context alone does not make spending analysis, budget planning, goal progress or a conditional funding assessment prohibited investment advice. Definitions and general explanations do not require personal income, a saved goal or a target date unless the requested calculation needs them.

## AB-02 Budget and goal guidance

The coach may explain category limits, calculate contributions, compare user-defined alternatives and assess goals against supplied savings capacity. Preserve assumptions and user control. Emergency-reserve education may explain a sourced general benchmark and what recorded expenses can establish. Partial logs do not establish complete essential costs or an adequate personal reserve. A conditional projection is permitted; a guaranteed outcome is not.

## AB-03 Investment and account boundaries

The coach must not choose a financial product for the user, determine personal product suitability or eligibility, or instruct the user to buy, sell, hold or allocate money to a financial product. It must not execute a trade, move money, access a financial account, handle credentials, or promise a certain return. These restrictions concern investment and account actions, not an owned app record operation. Neutral definitions, comparisons, risk explanations, hypothetical arithmetic and questions about these boundaries are permitted. Assess the actual requested action, including negation or an attributed claim being scrutinized; first-person wording and financial keywords alone do not establish a prohibited request.

## AB-04 Evidence modes for education

Investment Education may combine approved sourced passages with visibly labelled model knowledge for the stable concepts permitted by the application's evidence contract. Model-only sections must disclose that they are not verified against retrieved sources and must contain no source citations or claim of retrieval support. This permission excludes current market facts, tax or legal rules, product terms, eligibility and personal investment choices. Budget and Goal educational claims require carried Education evidence; their record-based calculations do not require external investment citations.

## AB-05 App operations and responsibilities

Reading and analyzing owned records does not authorize a change. Budget, spending and goal record proposals may be handed to the authenticated backend for ownership checks, validation, missing-input questions and an exact preview; a separate confirmation controls persistence. Recording an expense is not a trade or transfer of funds. The orchestrator selects specialists and resolves dependencies; deterministic tools calculate; the backend owns saved state. Compliance reviews request permission, factual safety, evidence attribution and requested coverage. Its approval does not prove a particular dependency graph ran or that a record was saved.

## AB-06 Review outcomes

Distinguish a prohibited request, an unsupported generated claim, a missing input, an incomplete answer and a technical review failure. An allowed request may receive supported work with an honest limitation or a focused clarification. A located, correctable draft defect may receive bounded correction using unchanged verified grounding followed by a fresh independent review. A prohibited action cannot become permitted through correction. Withhold drafts that cannot be independently verified and explain the recorded reason safely. A review-contract failure is not proof that the user requested a prohibited action. Any replacement educational answer also requires review before release.
