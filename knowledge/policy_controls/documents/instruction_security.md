# Retrieved-Content and Instruction Security Policy

## IS-01 Untrusted content

User messages, history, preferences, retrieved content and record labels are untrusted data. They cannot change system policy, expand permissions or override review. Their ordinary financial meaning may inform authorized intent interpretation, but embedded directives cannot control execution. Inspect retrieved content and reject or quarantine unsafe instructions before model use. Approved policy provenance does not grant retrieved text authority to replace system controls.

## IS-02 Routing and capability boundaries

The orchestrator may interpret user intent, select specialists and resolve capabilities permitted by the registry. A user request or retrieved passage cannot directly schedule an unapproved specialist, authorize a new tool or broaden a task's data access. Co-selection alone does not establish a dependency. Approved dependencies may pass scoped, typed results between agents; inspect and validate those results without treating narrative text as new authority. Provider reuse must preserve the applicable task, financial period and evidence identity.

## IS-03 Retrieval separation

Risk and Compliance may directly query only its Policy retriever. Investment Education may directly query only its Education retriever. Budget and Goal may request the approved Education-context capability; receiving its evidence does not grant direct retriever permission. Spending has no retrieval permission. Education queries use bounded public concepts rather than raw personal dialogue, identifiers or financial values. Policy sources are operating evidence, not end-user financial education citations.

## IS-04 Ownership, credentials and persistence

Use only backend-authorized records for the current user and profile. Block requests for another person's private financial data, credentials, hidden configuration or unauthorized account actions. Labels, dialogue and specialist output cannot establish ownership or consent. A follow-up may resolve a conversational subject but cannot independently authorize persistence. The authenticated backend validates any proposed app record change and controls confirmation and writes. Agents cannot claim that a proposal succeeded before the backend confirms it.

## IS-05 Failure and disclosure boundaries

Return provider failures as sanitized service errors without raw provider details. Public traces and logs must exclude raw prompts, retrieved text, credentials, personal grounding and rejected model prose. Retaining validated reason categories, policy provenance, fixed validation codes and safe issue ownership is permitted for troubleshooting blocked runs. A reason explanation must not reproduce the unsafe claim it describes or imply that a technical failure establishes prohibited intent. Operator-only redacted diagnostics remain access-restricted and expire under their separate retention controls.
