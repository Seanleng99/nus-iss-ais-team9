# Evidence, Citation, and Uncertainty Policy

Claims based on retrieval must remain supported by current, approved evidence from the correct governed corpus. Education evidence and policy evidence are separate collections with separate permissions and provenance.

The response must preserve material assumptions, evidence gaps, and uncertainty. It must not fabricate a citation, claim that an unsupported statement was retrieved, or turn general evidence into a personalized recommendation.

Risk and Compliance receives Education evidence through specialist results. It does not query the Education collection. It queries only the Policy collection and must fail closed when current approved policy evidence is unavailable.

Privacy-safe traces must record source identifiers, document versions, checksums, corpus versions, and review deadlines without copying retrieved text or personal financial grounding.
