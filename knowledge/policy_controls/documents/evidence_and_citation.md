# Evidence, Citation, and Uncertainty Policy

## EV-01 Evidence authority

Owned backend records establish what is saved or recorded, not independently verified real-world completeness. Deterministic tools establish calculations from those inputs. Attributed user statements may support explicitly illustrative calculations but are not saved records or confirmed balances. Approved Education passages support sourced educational claims; Policy passages define operating boundaries and never supply financial facts. An assistant statement, another agent's confidence, a source title or freshness metadata is not evidence for a financial claim.

## EV-02 Citation and passage support

Use current approved evidence from the correct governed corpus. Each citation must identify an actually carried source whose passage supports the claim and preserves its product scope and qualifications. A relevant keyword or source identifier alone is insufficient. Do not fabricate citations, misattribute a paraphrase, or describe model knowledge as retrieved evidence. Metadata may establish document provenance, not financial meaning. A faithful plain-language paraphrase is permitted.

## EV-03 Financial meanings and qualifications

Keep recorded savings balances, planned monthly savings capacity, unallocated income and income minus recorded expenses distinct. None alone proves cash available in a bank account or observed saving behavior. A confirmed savings allocation and an illustrative capacity estimate have different authority and assumptions. Preserve requested periods, currencies, methods, coverage limits and conditional conclusions. The app uses SGD for personal calculations; explicit non-SGD inputs require clarification, not conversion or relabelling. An educational source may retain its original currency. Do not assert a user's motives or a causal spending explanation from an observed association alone.

## EV-04 Periods, coverage and recurrence

Use the requested calendar scope; a default applies only when no period is specified. Missing records do not establish zero expenses, and partial records do not establish full household costs. Comparisons must disclose materially different observation windows. Forecast occurrences require explicit schedule terms; repeated descriptions alone do not establish recurrence. Projected obligations, recorded payments and unpaid occurrences have separate scopes. Preserve schedule uncertainty, deduplication boundaries and excluded currencies; do not infer the current state of separate workspace schedules from ledger rows.

## EV-05 Goal funding and combined answers

Saved-target arithmetic does not establish affordability. Concurrent goals consume one applicable monthly savings pool, not a fresh copy of that pool per goal. Keep unallocated income separate and state assumptions needed to sustain contributions. Preserve each specialist's evidence limits during composition and check conflicting amounts, dates, source claims and conclusions across sections. Evidence missing from one role does not establish that the combined answer lacks it. A handoff or a role label alone does not establish factual support.

## EV-06 Partial answers and conversation

Assess support and completeness per requested part. Retain useful supported calculations and explanations when another part lacks evidence. An honest limitation may answer an excluded or current-scope question; it must not falsely claim that an unsupported fact is known. Ask only for inputs relevant to the requested calculation. History may resolve a subject and preserve attributed corrections or preferences; it is not proof of saved state, payment or consent. Current requests take precedence over older presentation preferences. Language, layout and detail preferences must preserve verified amounts, scope, citations and limitations.

## EV-07 Evidence separation and availability

Compliance receives Education evidence through specialist results and directly queries only Policy. Investment Education alone has direct Education-retriever permission; Budget and Goal may receive that evidence through authorized orchestrator capabilities. Missing optional Education support does not invalidate separately grounded arithmetic. Approved model-only education remains subject to its explicit limits. Current approved Policy evidence is required for model-assisted compliance review; if it is unavailable, withhold the answer and identify policy-evidence unavailability rather than an established user violation.

## EV-08 Review explanations and diagnostics

A blocking review must identify its category and actual defect. Where applicable, identify the affected section, corrective action and supporting evidence references. Keep permission, factual safety and completeness separate. Invalid review contracts are technical verification failures. Public explanations may render validated categories and policy-clause references using reviewed application wording. Privacy-safe traces retain source identifiers, versions, checksums, corpus versions, review deadlines and fixed validation codes, without copying rejected text, raw prompts or personal grounding. Redacted operator-only diagnostics have separate access and retention controls; they are not public trace content.
