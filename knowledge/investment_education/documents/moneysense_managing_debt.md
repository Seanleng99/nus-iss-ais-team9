Managing debt
=============

Project-authored educational paraphrase of the public source below; this is not a reproduction of the source document.

Debt can grow through interest and late charges, while scheduled repayments reduce the cash available for other needs. As a general prioritisation principle, addressing higher-interest debt first can limit interest costs; this is not an individual repayment plan. Missing payments may also trigger fees or other consequences under the agreement. If repayments are becoming difficult, contact the financial institution early to discuss the situation. Credit Counselling Singapore may also provide guidance. The appropriate options depend on a person’s circumstances and the terms of each debt.
