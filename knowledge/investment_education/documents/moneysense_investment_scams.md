Spotting investment scams
=========================

Project-authored educational paraphrase of the public source below; this is not a reproduction of the source document.

Promises of high returns with little or no risk are a warning sign. Check an entity’s permitted activities in the MAS Financial Institutions Directory and verify representatives in the MAS Financial Institution Representatives Register. Independently find official contact details and confirm that a person or account really represents the named institution; scammers may impersonate genuine firms. Pressure to act, referral rewards or positive testimonials do not establish that an offer is legitimate. A directory check is one safeguard, not a guarantee, and this summary does not report a live provider check or determine legitimacy from an anecdote.
