Compound interest
=================

Project-authored educational paraphrase of the public source below; this is not a reproduction of the source document.

Compound interest means interest can accrue on both the original amount and earlier interest. For illustration, $100 growing at a constant 4% annual rate would become $104 after one year and $108.16 after two years. This is a hypothetical calculation, not a forecast or suggested return; it assumes the rate stays constant and ignores fees and taxes. The same compounding effect can increase debt when interest is added to an unpaid balance. Actual outcomes depend on the product, terms, charges and payment behaviour.
